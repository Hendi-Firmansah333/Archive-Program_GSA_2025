## 👋 About My Journey

Welcome to my **Google Student Ambassador (GSA) 2025** archive!

My name is **Hendi Firmansah**, an Information Technology student at **Politeknik Negeri Lampung, Indonesia**.

The Google Student Ambassador program was a valuable part of my journey as a university student. Through this experience, I had the opportunity to grow, explore new technologies, and contribute to introducing artificial intelligence and Google products to the campus community.

For me, this program was more than just an achievement. It was an opportunity to begin my journey as a student who wants to bring positive change to campus by encouraging others to explore technology, especially AI, in the digital era.

I believe that technology becomes more meaningful when knowledge is shared and opportunities to learn are accessible to more people.

## 🎯 My Mission

During my GSA journey, I was motivated to:

- Introduce AI and Google technologies to fellow students.
- Encourage students to explore new digital tools and innovations.
- Share knowledge and create opportunities for technology learning.
- Support a campus environment that embraces digital transformation.
- Develop communication, collaboration, leadership, and organizational skills.
